Three rules baked in

  1. No nouns that turn into text. Generators render the word “sign”, “label” or “brand” as lettering on the image. None of these prompts use them.
  2. Every person gets ethnicity, age, build and a stated expression. Leave the expression out and you get blank mannequin faces.
  3. Every number plate is described as blank. Otherwise you get gibberish characters that read as fake at a glance.

Before you accept an image

  1. No gibberish text anywhere, and no characters on the number plate.
  2. No badge shape that reads as a real manufacturer.
  3. Five fingers per hand, no warped reflections or bent body panels.
  4. The grade is gold on black — if there is blue in the shadows, run it again.
